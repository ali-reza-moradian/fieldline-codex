Market estimatesMargin · fair chance · estimated EV

These are price-based estimates, not predictions. Fair chance removes each book’s implied margin, then averages the two books. Estimated EV combines that chance with the best available price. Estimates assume a settled win or loss; pushes and voids are excluded. Neither guarantees a profitable bet.

Implied margin adds the best prices’ implied chances and subtracts 100%. A negative value marks an arbitrage candidate only: both sides, point line, period and settlement must agree. Availability, limits, voids and final settlement can change. Missing, stale or paused prices are excluded. Extra player markets are not included in these estimates.
